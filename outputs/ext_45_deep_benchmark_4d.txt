====================================================================================================
  P1  the costs, counted rather than estimated, in the project's two units
====================================================================================================

  The cost unit is FORWARD EVALUATIONS, and both paths are counted in it exactly:

    deep arm    SPSA '2 * spsa_avg forward passes' per epoch per network
                = 2 * 60 epochs * 3 layers * 2 networks = **720 forwards**
    shallow     `fit_operator` returns `n_evals` -- it charges the selection
                evaluations, 'because a restart that is not paid for is not a method'

  **AND A SECOND UNIT THAT MUST NOT BE ADDED TO THE FIRST:** the closed-form solve
  costs `n_train * N` READINGS (`readout_calibration_cost`).  The shallow arm pays it
  and the deep arm does not, so reporting a single combined number would flatter one
  side.  Both are reported side by side throughout.

====================================================================================================
  P3  VERDICT: is the deep path competitive per forward?
====================================================================================================

  deep arm:            +0.0548 at 720 forwards
  shallow at or under: +0.5817 at 408 forwards
  shallow first beats it at 104 forwards (+0.2626)

  **THE DEEP PATH IS NOT MERELY UNCOMPETITIVE -- IT IS BEATEN BY THE NULL.**
  A ONE-LAYER shallow arm scores `+0.5817` at `408` forwards, and
  the RANDOM-GEOMETRY null -- which spends **ZERO forwards** and only `20`
  readings -- scores `+0.2516`, against the deep arm's `+0.0548` at 3x the
  particles and 720 forwards.  **An earlier draft of this verdict said only
  'not competitive', which UNDERSTATES the measurement**, and the sharper
  statement is the one the table supports: on the repository's own regression
  task, three layers of 20 nodes are beaten by one layer of 20 nodes, and by
  picking the geometry at random.

  **AND THAT IS NOT A STRAW-MAN NULL.**  Random geometry plus a closed-form solve
  is the extension's own standard baseline, used in every half-B result, and it
  is exactly what the project's central claim predicts should be strong: the
  strengths are SOLVED, so the only thing left to get right is the geometry --
  and on a 2-D task, random geometry is already good.

  **THE SCOPE, AND ONE CLAIM THAT THE SECOND TASK REFUTED.**  An earlier draft of
  this verdict said the deep path 'exists for the harder problems in this
  repository -- the 4-D and image lines', i.e. that an EASY task is where it
  loses.  **`--task 4d` refutes that:** the deep arm scores `+0.0548` there --
  barely over the floor -- against the zero-forward null's `+0.2516` and a
  one-layer `N=40` arm's `+0.5817`.  **It is beaten by the null on the HARDER task
  too, and by a wider relative margin.**  The claim is withdrawn rather than kept
  as a caveat, because a caveat that the data contradicts is worse than no caveat.

  **WHAT THE SCOPE LIMIT ACTUALLY IS.**  Toy scale; one deep configuration whose
  hyper-parameters were inherited from a pipeline test and never tuned; and the
  repository's own two REGRESSION tasks.  **The image line is CLASSIFICATION, whose
  readout is nearest-prototype and is not what this script compares** -- so nothing
  here says anything about it either way, and that is the one place a deep design
  still has room in this repository.

  **AND WHAT THIS BENCHMARK IS NOT.**  It compares ONE deep configuration against ONE
  shallow family on the repository's 2d task, at toy scale, with the deep arm's own
  hyper-parameters (`nd_04`'s lr and eps).  **The deep arm's hyper-parameters were
  never tuned** -- they were inherited from a pipeline test -- so this is a benchmark
  of the path as it ships, not of what it could reach.

  **THE HONEST LIMITS.**  `n_evals` is the shallow arm's own accounting; the deep
  arm's is analytic from SPSA's documented cost.  Neither counts the feature-matrix
  evaluations inside the closed-form solve, because those are readings rather than
  forwards and are reported in the other column.  Repository task, 3 seeds, toy.
