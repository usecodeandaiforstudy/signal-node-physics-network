    DEVICE     produces Gaussian nodes; it does not know the target
    NETWORK    40 Gaussian nodes with a linear readout

  basis limitation is expected.  The question is whether it is the whole
  story: if best-found is also ~0 then no placement can fix `steps` and the
  operator's deficit there is second order; if best-found is clearly
  positive, the placement deficit is the finding.
