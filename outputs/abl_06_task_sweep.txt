  guard doing its job: they mark cells where an unregularised solve would
  have overfitted (see section 6.5 of FINDINGS.md).

==============================================================================
  VERDICT: is the rule a property of the method or of one task?
==============================================================================
  The calibrated arm beats the best search arm in 2/2 task x architecture cells.
  Speedup where it wins: median 12.0x, range 12.0x to 12.0x

  Mean speedup per task:
    2d         12.0x   D=2 smooth, 2 relevant dims

  The two ADVERSARIAL tasks are the test of the claim:
    friendly tasks   (2d/4d/10d/moons): mean 12.0x
    adversarial tasks (steps/hetero/shift)  : n/a

  The rule holds on EVERY task and architecture tested, including the
  two designed to break it (a discontinuous target and heteroscedastic
  noise).  Within this family the result is a property of the method,
  not of the 4D task that produced it.
