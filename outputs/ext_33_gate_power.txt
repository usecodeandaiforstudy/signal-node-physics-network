  Section 6.11 tried three counterexamples and all failed, and it says why the
  third one could not settle the question: a FIRST-ORDER failure is detectable at
  any slice size, so it never probed the gate's STATISTICAL POWER.  That needs a
  task near the decision boundary, and the base says it was never constructed.

  THE GATE: val = every `guard_holdout`-th training sample (a STRIDE); fit the
  strengths on the complement; keep iff the fitted strengths BEAT the in-place
  ones on that stride, strictly.  A strict inequality on a finite sample is
  exactly what luck can carry at the boundary, and `guard_holdout` is a direct
  knob on how many samples decide it.

  THE CONFOUND IN THIS COMPARISON, stated because it bounds the verdict: the
  hybrid and geometry arms are separate trainings, matched on data, seed and
  initialisation, but the calibration CHANGES THE TRAJECTORY -- it rewrites
  W_gauss mid-run, so the finite differences that follow see a different loss
  surface.  So `hybrid < geometry` is the calibration's effect PLUS that
  divergence, and a single cell cannot separate them.  The base flagged the same
  conflation in its own section 6.11 and this design narrows it without closing it.

  CELLS WITH A NEGATIVE MARGIN AND AN ACCEPTED CALIBRATION:
    noise=1.5 holdout=4: accepted 3, hybrid - geometry -0.0283

  **THE COUNTEREXAMPLE EXISTS: 1 of 11 boundary cells
  had the gate ACCEPT a calibration and the arm still end BELOW its own
  geometry arm.**  That is the evidence section 6.11 searched for and did not
  find, and it is the guarantee -- not the stronger 'beats the best search
  arm' claim -- that it bears on.

  AND THE POWER IS SLICE-SIZE-DEPENDENT: the accept rate moves by 0.483
  across guard_holdout.  So the gate's decision at the boundary IS partly a
  matter of how many samples decide it -- which is the property section 6.11
  said it had never tested.

  SCOPE: ONE task whose noise level was swept, one architecture, diffusion
  substrate, the base's own effective_budget, 40 epochs and the seeds above.
  A single boundary is not the boundary: a different task could sit elsewhere and
  behave differently, and nothing here bounds that.
