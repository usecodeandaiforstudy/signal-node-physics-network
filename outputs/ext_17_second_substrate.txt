  The operator suite was circuit-only until now, and could not be otherwise:
  `features` hard-coded make_layer("circuit") and had no substrate
  parameter.  A thesis whose main clause is 'any physical system' rested on
  one substrate for the part of the argument that trains the network.

  PREDICTION, stated before measuring: in log space the per-source amplitude
  enters ONLY the constant term, and OP-G recovers h from alpha and c from
  gamma without ever using that constant -- so the geometry operator MUST be
  invariant to it.  Falsifiable, and the reason this is more than coverage.

  The measurement protocol the operators assume, stated because it is an
  assumption: ONE node is driven at a time with the others off.  Under that
  protocol the diffusion cell's PU normalisation does not enter the reading
  (the normalising sum is the driven node alone), so PU is not a confound
  here -- and a protocol that drove all sources at once would need the
  coupling term accounted for, which nothing in this extension does.

========================================================================================
  P1  THE PREDICTION: is the geometry operator invariant to per-source amplitude?
========================================================================================

  per-source amplitude 1/g_j over 12 nodes: min 1.571e+07  max 1.650e+08  ratio 1.050e+01
  (it depends on the node's own width, since g ~ t^(dim/2) and t ~ h^2)

                            quantity      unit peak   field (rescaled)   difference
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
        centre difference (absolute)                                      2.358e-15
         width difference (relative)                                      3.418e-16
              consistency difference                                      3.975e-01

  READ THE CONSISTENCY ROW AS A CONFIRMATION, not as a failure.  The operator
  checks delta = |gamma|^2/(4 alpha), and the amplitude is absorbed by delta --
  which is exactly where the prediction says it must go.  So the amplitude
  moves the CONSISTENCY RESIDUAL and leaves the GEOMETRY untouched, and that
  is a sharper statement of the mechanism than the invariance alone:

    A CONSISTENCY GATE REJECTS AN UNCALIBRATED MEASUREMENT OF A PERFECTLY
    RECOVERABLE NODE.  The geometry is right to 1e-15 while the check that is
    supposed to validate the measurement moves by 0.4.  Any hardware procedure
    that gates on consistency must therefore calibrate the amplitude FIRST, or
    it will discard good readings -- which is the opposite of what a validity
    check is for.
          nodes the operator refused                                              0

  THE PREDICTION HOLDS.  A per-source amplitude spanning 1e+01x leaves the
  recovered centre and width unchanged to numerical precision, because
  the amplitude is a per-node constant and OP-G never uses the constant
  term.  The geometry operators are therefore substrate-AGNOSTIC BY
  CONSTRUCTION, not by coincidence -- which is the strongest form the
  'any physical system' claim can take.

  THE SUITE TRANSFERS.  The kernel identity holds exactly on the diffusion
  cell, the geometry operators are provably insensitive to the one thing

  full trainer still beats the null with the chain engaged.  The 'any

  WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH, stated so the claim is not read wider than
  it is: two substrates is not 'any'.  Both cells were built here to
  reproduce the same engineered kernel, so this shows the operators do not
  depend on which of the two produced the field -- not that they would
  survive a substrate whose response is not Gaussian in the input at all.
  The prediction says exactly what the operators need: log-linearity in
  [-|x|^2, x, -1].  A substrate that breaks THAT breaks the operators, and
  no cell here tests that.
