  PAIRED comparison.  The arms see the SAME data seeds, so the correct
  statistic is the per-seed difference (k minus full), not the difference
  of means against the across-seed sd -- the latter is dominated by which
  data split was drawn and makes the test unable to fail.
  k=4: paired gap +0.0006 (sd 0.0005, sem 0.0003 over 2 seeds)  tol 0.0200  OK
  k=3: paired gap -0.0005 (sd 0.0045, sem 0.0032 over 2 seeds)  tol 0.0200  OK

  boundary: largest tested k < m is 4; smallest PASSING k is 3

  Does the constraint's cost GROW as the geometry is trained?  A paired gap
  that widens would mean the geometry and the rank constraint compete; one
  that stays flat or narrows means they do not.

   epoch      k=4 gap     k=3 gap
  ------------------------------
       1      +0.0010     -0.0031
      31      +0.0004     -0.0032
      60      +0.0006     -0.0005

  SURVIVES at every k tested, and the geometry demonstrably moved, so this
  is not a re-measurement of the fixed-geometry case.  Combined with
  ext_02 this is the representational case for the extension: the saving
  is free both at a fixed geometry and under geometry training.
  The boundary lies below k = 3 and was not probed here.
