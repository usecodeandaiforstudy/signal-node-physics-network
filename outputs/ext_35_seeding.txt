  global RNG, so two calls with identical arguments gave different networks.
  The fix seeds once at the top of `train`, which is sufficient because the
  class has exactly two randomness sources and both hang off `train`:
      `_init_layer`  -- centres, W_gauss, W_act   (3x np.random.uniform)
      `_spsa_step`   -- the SPSA perturbation      (1x np.random.choice)
