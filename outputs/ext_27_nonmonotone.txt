  THE RIDGE SCALE IS THE MECHANISM: a lambda the base does not use removes the
  dip in BOTH families, and the base's own 1e-3 is the only value on this grid
  that produces it.  So the non-monotonicity is a mis-scaled ridge and it is
  FIXABLE rather than intrinsic -- and the base already carries the fix, since
  `closed_form_lam="auto"` chooses the scale on held-out data.

  BUT THE RESCUE IS DEMONSTRATED FOR A DIFFERENT QUANTITY THAN THE ONE 12.10
  AND 12.11 MEASURED, and that gap is stated rather than glossed:

    P2 reports the HYBRID's R^2 at the common FRONTIER, because that is what
    the last curve point is.  12.10 and 12.11 reported the LOW-BUDGET MARGIN at
    3000 measurements, hybrid minus best search.  The search arms do not use
    `closed_form_lam` at all, so their R^2 is unchanged by this sweep -- but the
    @3000 MARGIN AT lam=auto is NOT measured here.

  **So this script establishes that the frontier collapse is a ridge-scale
  artefact and NOT that the low-budget advantage survives at lam=auto.**  The
  second needs the same @3000 comparison recomputed at auto, and it is the
  obvious next run rather than a conclusion of this one.
