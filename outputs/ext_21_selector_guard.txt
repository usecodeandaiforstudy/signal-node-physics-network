  ext_21 - a guard for the restart selector
  nodes=40 seeds=3 M=16

  ext_19 showed `restarts=M` reaches the ceiling AND selected -7.5180 on
  `steps` and -0.4235 on the SMOOTH control.  A repair that is dangerous at
  some M cannot be used, so the item it opened is a guard.

  THIS SCRIPT DOES NOT PICK A GUARD AND JUSTIFY IT AFTERWARDS.  It measures
  what distinguishes the catastrophic restarts, guards on that, and then checks
  the guard does not reject good models.  The pool is run ONCE per (task, seed)
  and every selector is evaluated on the SAME pool, so guarded vs unguarded is a
  paired comparison rather than two draws.

============================================================================================
  P1  what distinguishes a catastrophic restart?
============================================================================================

  A restart is called catastrophic if its test R^2 is more than 0.05 below the
  seed-0 single run's, i.e. selection made things WORSE than not restarting.

  task     statistic               median: bad       good   separates?
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  steps    pred spread / target          0.674      0.778           no
  steps    weight norm                    7.98        8.3           no
  steps    feature condition               535        176          YES

  4d       pred spread / target          0.694      0.693           no
  4d       weight norm                      14       12.5           no
  4d       feature condition              13.7         13           no

  ALL THREE statistics are reported, not just the one the mechanism predicted.
  The hypothesis behind the prediction-spread guard was that a large weight on a
  wide node makes a large CONSTANT prediction that explodes outside the basis
  support.  If NO statistic separates, the mechanism is wrong and the guard has
  to come from somewhere else -- which is what the numbers above decide.

  steps: worst restart test -22.4165, prediction spread 1.373x the target's, weight norm 161.9, cond 7.52e+06
  4d: worst restart test -2.4421, prediction spread 0.843x the target's, weight norm 37.2, cond 9.54e+06

============================================================================================
  P1b if no statistic separates: is this the WINNER'S CURSE instead?
============================================================================================

  Selection takes the MAXIMUM of M held-out estimates, each computed on a
  validation split of about 51 samples.  The maximum of noisy estimates is
  biased upward and need not correspond to a good model, and the bias grows
  with M -- which would explain why the repair got WORSE from M=4 to M=16 while
  a larger M can only help if the criterion were informative.

  task      val of selected   mean val     curse  test of selected   mean test
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  steps             +0.3909    +0.2253   +0.1656           -7.5181     -0.3404
  4d                +0.6166    +0.5003   +0.1163           +0.5910     +0.4859

  'curse' is the selected restart's held-out score minus the pool's MEAN
  held-out score: how much the maximum flatters itself.  If the selected
  restart's TEST score is far below the pool's MEAN test score, selection is
  picking badly and the criterion -- not the guard -- is what needs replacing.

============================================================================================
  VERDICT: can the selector be made safe without losing the ceiling?
============================================================================================

  steps    unguarded mean -7.5181 worst -22.4165
           no guard tested lifts the worst case without losing the mean
  4d       unguarded mean +0.5910 worst +0.5004
           no guard tested lifts the worst case without losing the mean

  what separated, stated precisely: the prediction-spread guard is REFUTED
  (0.674 against 0.778 on `steps` -- the catastrophic restarts are if anything
  TIGHTER), the weight norm does not separate on either task, and the feature
  condition number separates on `steps` (535 against 176) but NOT on `4d`
  (13.7 against 13).  So one statistic does separate, but
  only on the task it was measured on -- which is a post-hoc guard, not a
  principled one, and it is not adopted.

  AND P1b SAYS WHY NO GUARD WAS GOING TO WORK.  On `steps` the selected restart
  scores -7.5182 on test while the POOL'S MEAN is -0.3404: selection is not
  merely noisy, it is picking worse than a random member of its own pool.  The
  winner's curse alone is only +0.1657 of held-out flattery and cannot explain a
  gap of that size.

  THAT TIES BACK TO ext_19's MECHANISM FINDING, and the two together are the
  real result: on `steps` the proposal family wins only 42.5% of grow steps
  against an equal-sized random pool (chance is ~50%), so the runs differ by
  NOISE rather than by quality, and a 51-sample held-out split cannot rank
  noise.  A restart repair can only work where the proposals carry signal; on
  the task where they do not, more restarts make selection WORSE, and no guard
  on the resulting model can put back a signal the criterion never had.

  NO GUARD TESTED WORKS, so `restarts=M` REMAINS UNUSABLE and is reported as
  such: the ceiling is reachable (ext_19's M=4 found it) but the selector
  cannot be trusted to find it, and a repair that is dangerous at some M is
  not a repair.  `restarts=1` stays the default, and the parameter is
  documented as unsafe above 1 rather than shipped as a fix.

  THE DEFECT IS THEREFORE UPSTREAM, in the PROPOSAL FAMILY on discontinuous
  targets -- not in the selector, and not in the number of restarts.  That is
  a sharper place to look than where this item started, and it is the honest
  outcome of a guard that was measured rather than chosen.
